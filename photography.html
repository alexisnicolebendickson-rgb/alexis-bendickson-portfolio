Photography

Before the archive, there was the lens.

From 2014 to 2018 I ran an independent photography studio serving more than 200 clients — portraits, families, and visual storytelling across Missouri and Kansas.

Photography taught me to look carefully — a skill I now bring to archival documents. Running the studio meant managing everything: client relationships, contracts, scheduling, marketing, and brand development, alongside the creative work itself.

These days the camera comes out for research trips, landscapes, and family — including the golden-hour portraits below, all shot and edited on iPhone.
