Speaking

I speak about the record — and who it left out.

Talks and workshops on archival research, public history, and the nontraditional path to the archive — for classrooms, conferences, community groups, and libraries.

Talk topics

What I speak about

Public history

Beyond Avalon: Reclaiming the Record

How archival research recovers the names and lives the official record tried to erase — the methods, the dead ends, and what the documents actually say. Built around my first-place SCS research.

Workshop

Archives for Everyone

A practical, beginner-friendly workshop on doing genealogical and archival research without a university behind you: where the records live, how to read them, and how to keep a research log that holds up.

Education

Unlearning the Empire

The lens behind my 185-episode series: how history, policy, and power connect — and how ordinary people can learn to read them together.

Keynote

The Nontraditional Path

Poverty, chronic illness, motherhood, a heart attack and a coma — and getting to the archive anyway. For student audiences who don't see themselves in academia yet.
